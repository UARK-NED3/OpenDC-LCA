# Governance

OpenDC-LDA begins as a University of Arkansas–hosted research project.

## Decision principles

- Scientific validity and transparent provenance take priority over convenience.
- Maintainers disclose relevant financial and organizational conflicts.
- Vendor-neutral schemas and benchmarks are preferred.
- Technology comparisons receive review from contributors without a direct stake
  in the evaluated product whenever practical.
- Breaking methodological changes require a documented decision record.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** review code, methods, and releases.
- **Data stewards** verify provenance, licensing, and metadata.
- **Domain reviewers** assess thermal, reliability, water, and LCA assumptions.

Roles and named maintainers will be formalized after the founding collaborators
agree on scope. Until then, repository administrators are interim maintainers.

## Releases

Software uses semantic versioning. Benchmark datasets are versioned separately
and must remain citable. Published studies should record the code commit,
scenario files, database versions, and data releases used.
