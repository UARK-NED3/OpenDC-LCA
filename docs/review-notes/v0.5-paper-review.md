# v0.5 scientific review notes

These notes preserve questions and provisional responses for later review by
Dr. Darin Nutter. They are not requests for immediate answers.

## Is the v0.4 result a full reproduction?

**Provisional response:** No. It establishes arithmetic consistency and
traceability for the released Figure 4 component table. It must be described as
a public-result reproduction audit because licensed and confidential
background inventories cannot be independently reconstructed.

## Can the v0.5 hourly percentages be reported as technology findings?

**Provisional response:** No. They are conditional consequences of transparent
PUE hypotheses. They become comparative findings only after the performance
curves are estimated from representative measurements and their uncertainty is
propagated.

## Is annual eGRID appropriate for hourly analysis?

**Provisional response:** It is acceptable for demonstrating hourly cooling
energy integration, but it does not create hourly carbon accounting. A
decision-grade extension should use hourly average or marginal factors selected
to match the study goal.

## Is constant hourly IT load acceptable?

**Provisional response:** Only as an explicitly labeled baseline. Load profiles
and temperature-load interactions should be added before drawing operational
conclusions.

## Should the functional unit remain IT MWh?

**Provisional response:** IT MWh is suitable for isolating cooling energy, but
comparison with the Microsoft study requires a compute-service unit such as
annualized virtual cores or a measured workload-output unit. Both should be
reported where possible.

## Which result should enter a manuscript abstract?

**Provisional response:** The exact reconciliation of 24 released totals is a
defensible reproducibility result. The hourly percentages should appear only as
protocol-demonstration results until measured performance maps are available.

## What review would authorize comparative claims?

**Provisional response:** Review goal and scope, functional equivalence,
inventory mapping, uncertainty, scenario representativeness, and
interpretation. Public comparative assertions may also require an independent
critical-review panel consistent with ISO 14044.
