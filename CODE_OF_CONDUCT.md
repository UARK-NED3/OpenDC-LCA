# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-driven research
community. Be constructive, distinguish evidence from opinion, disclose
conflicts of interest, respect confidentiality and data licenses, and never
misrepresent illustrative data as measured results.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository administrators.
Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to
protect the community and scientific integrity.
